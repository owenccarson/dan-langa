About


Dan Langa is a Northampton, MA-based composer, producer, recording and mix engineer primarily focused on electroacoustic music that explores vivid, bold textures. A collaborative spirit, Langa often works across mediums and genres, teaming up with other artists in film, dance, and other areas of music to create multimedia works.

Langa’s recent projects emphasize these strong interests in cross-disciplinary collaboration. Recenty releases include After Nothing Comes under the moniker Fugue State released on Switch Hit Records in 2026. Created in collaboration with Grammy Award-winning producer and New Amsterdam Records Co-Founder William Brittelle, it takes its inspiration from artist Aidan Koch’s 2016 zine After Nothing Comes. Like Langa’s compositions, Koch’s drawings explore the contours of texture, evoking dream-like moods and landscapes. In 2024-25, Langa served as a So Percussion Studio Resident with the electroacoustic trio Other Faces. This trio, comprised of Kristian de Leon, Adam Lutz, and Langa, and released their debut, self-titled album on cmntx records in November 2026.


A sought-after producer, engineer, and composer for film and television, Langa works primarily out of The Honey Jar recording studio in Dumbo, Brooklyn.

Langa has received awards, grants, and recognition from the ASCAP Foundation, Millay Arts, Ucross Foundation, KHN Center for the Arts, Avaloch Farm, I-Park Foundation, Fire Island National Seashore,, Du Vert à L'Infini Festival, RED NOTE New Music Festival, Hot Air Music Festival, So Percussion Summer Institute, Bang On A Can Summer Festival, Fresh Inc Festival, The Ear Competition, Amherst College, New York University, and the University of South Carolina Spark Laboratory.

Langa is the co-founder of cmntx records, a New York City-based and artist-run record label committed to supporting the music and careers of artists with distinct, multifaceted outputs. cmntx records was founded in 2021 along with Alex Ring Gray, Evan O. Adams, and Michael Denis Ó Callaghan.

Langa earned his BA in Music from Amherst College, graduating magna cum laude with highest departmental honors studying with Eric Sawyer, John McDonald, Bruce Diehl, Eric Wubbels, and Ryan Vigil. He was awarded the Eric Edward Sundquist Prize for excellence in musical composition and performance. Langa received his Master’s Degree in Music Theory and Composition from New York University studying with Julia Wolfe and Robert Honstein. He has held teaching positions at Amherst College, Mount Holyoke College, and Smith College.
