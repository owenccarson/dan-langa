Projects

Fugue State

Led by composer and producer Dan Langa, Fugue State tears music apart in search of ghosts. Manipulated recordings turn into spectral collages — saxophone ambient meets crumbling art song meets elegantly fried motherboard remixes. A process of hunting for phantom sounds, tearing apart the house – an aural Gordon Matta-Clark. The haunting, then the hunting.

Other Faces

A multimedia electroacoustic trio fusing visual and auditory textures into radiant, expansive work. Bound by a shared curiosity to push tonality into textural and visual territory, the trio builds its music through sonic exploration and meticulous electronic crafting.

cmntx records

A New York City-based label committed to supporting artists with distinctive, multifaceted outputs. cmntx was founded in 2021 by four composers whose backgrounds didn't simply fit into just the "classical" tradition, and sought to help document the work of other non-traditional artists in our community. Our projects often cross lines and limitations of genre, aesthetic, and medium.
